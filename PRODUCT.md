# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed by Favour):

- Hiring managers and engineering leads deciding whether to interview Favour for a full-time role. They arrive from a CV or LinkedIn link and scan quickly.
- Founders and businesses deciding whether to pay Favour to build their product. They want proof that he has shipped something like what they need.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Favour Israel Taiwo, a software engineer in Lagos, Nigeria. It exists to turn a visit into a conversation: an interview request or a project enquiry. Success is a visitor opening a project, then making contact.

## Positioning

One engineer who ships the whole product (confirmed by Favour, all four apply):

- Builds both the web app (Next.js, React) and the mobile app (React Native, Expo) for the same product.
- The work is live products with real users, not exercises.
- Has built AI and WhatsApp tooling (AISHA analytics, BroadcastHub, the AI chat on this site).
- Owns frontend, backend (Node.js, Go), database and deployment.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from a CV, LinkedIn, GitHub or a direct link, often on a phone.
- The site has a homepage, a projects index, a page per project, a contact page, and an AI chat that answers questions about Favour.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, Tailwind v3, TypeScript, deployed on Vercel.
- 14 project write-ups exist in `content/projects/*.mdx` (title, description, date, optional live URL and repository).
- AI chat backed by Groq (`app/api/chat`), contact form backed by Resend (`app/api/contact`), page-view counts in Upstash Redis.
- An unfinished, uncommitted earlier redesign (Prisma CMS, `/admin`) is in the working tree. Whether to keep it is undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Favour Israel Taiwo. Site title currently "fiittech.com".
- No binding visual constraints were given.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project screenshots: Favour has them, not yet in the repository.
- Project write-ups in `content/projects/`.
- Absent, do not fabricate: testimonials, user counts, metrics, a portrait photo.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the proof. Show real screens of real products before any claim.
2. Serve the 10-second scan and the 5-minute read. Both audiences must get their answer.
3. Web and mobile are shown together, because building both is the differentiator.
4. Claim only what is true. No invented numbers, clients or quotes.
5. Every page has one obvious way to make contact.
