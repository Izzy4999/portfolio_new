The **Umley** app is a comprehensive tenant management platform designed to streamline property-related transactions and communications. Key features include:

- **Bill and Rent Payments**: Tenants can easily make payments for their house bills and rent directly through the app, ensuring timely and convenient transactions.
- **Electricity Token Purchase**: Instantly purchase electricity tokens, receive them via SMS, and track monthly usage with a user-friendly dashboard.
- **Maintenance Requests**: Users can submit maintenance requests with photos directly from the app, making it easy to report issues and track their resolution status in real-time.
- **Document Upload**: Tenants can upload and manage important documents such as lease agreements, receipts, and other property-related paperwork in one secure location.
- **Smart Financial Tools**: View transaction history, track balances, and use multiple payment methods with a digital wallet.
- **Utility Management**: Monitor consumption, manage diesel payments, and view token history.
- **Secure Access**: Biometric login, encrypted data, and emergency contacts ensure your data and payments are always protected.

This app simplifies property management for tenants, providing a centralized platform for all housing-related needs.

Available on [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ng/app/umley/id6748885939) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.umley.umley).
