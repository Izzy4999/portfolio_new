The **Teacher Guide App** is a comprehensive platform designed for educators to create, manage, and organize courses seamlessly. With this app, users can:

- **Create Courses**: Set up new courses with a user-friendly interface.
- **Add Course Materials**: Upload and manage various types of learning resources such as documents, videos, and assignments.
- **PIN-based Login**: Secure access to the app via a personalized PIN, ensuring data privacy and convenience.

This app is ideal for streamlining course management and enhancing the educational experience.
