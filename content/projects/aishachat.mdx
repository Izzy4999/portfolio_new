**AISHA Chat** brings the AISHA AI health companion to the browser as an installable progressive web app. It gives people the same conversational health assistant they know from WhatsApp, in an app they can add straight to their home screen.

## Phone-Number Sign-In

There are no passwords. Users enter their phone number, receive a verification code, and they are in. The flow defaults to Nigerian numbers (+234), matching where most AISHA users are.

## Built for the Phone First

The interface is designed at phone width first, so it feels native when installed from the browser to a home screen.

## Health Data Handled Responsibly

Health data in the app is handled under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), and users accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy when they continue.

## Key Features

- **AI Health Chat**: a conversational assistant for health questions
- **OTP Login**: phone number plus a one-time code, no passwords
- **Installable PWA**: add to home screen for app-like use
- **Mobile-First Layout**: designed for phone screens from the start
