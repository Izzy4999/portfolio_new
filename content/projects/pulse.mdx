**Pulse** is a powerful survey as a service platform designed to streamline the entire survey lifecycle from creation to analysis. Built for organizations that need to gather insights, feedback, and data from their users, customers, or stakeholders.

## Create Surveys with Ease

Pulse provides an intuitive interface for creating comprehensive surveys. Whether you need customer feedback, employee satisfaction surveys, market research, or event evaluations, Pulse makes it simple to build professional surveys with various question types including multiple choice, text responses, ratings, and more.

## Distribute Surveys Seamlessly

Once your survey is ready, Pulse offers multiple distribution channels to reach your target audience. Share surveys via direct links, email campaigns, embedded widgets, or QR codes. The platform ensures your surveys reach the right people at the right time.

## Collect and View Responses in Real-Time

As users fill out your surveys, responses are collected instantly and displayed in real-time dashboards. Monitor completion rates, track response trends, and gain immediate insights into your data. No waiting, no delays—see what your audience is saying as they say it.

## Comprehensive Analytics and Reporting

Pulse goes beyond simple data collection. The platform provides detailed analytics, visualizations, and reporting tools to help you understand your data. Export results, generate reports, and make data-driven decisions with confidence.

## Key Features

- **Survey Builder**: Intuitive drag-and-drop interface for creating surveys
- **Multiple Question Types**: Support for various question formats
- **Real-Time Analytics**: View responses and insights as they come in
- **Distribution Tools**: Multiple channels to share and distribute surveys
