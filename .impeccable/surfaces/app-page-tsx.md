# Portfolio public site (home, projects, project page, contact, AI chat)

Scope: all public routes. Mode: Experience. Audience: hiring managers and founders arriving from a CV or LinkedIn link, often on a phone. Job: open a project, then make contact. Constraints: light only (direction pinned by Favour to the Silk Canopy picture), real screenshots only where the write-ups link them, no invented metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sunlit courtyard under three silk banners; Favour's products sit in the light underneath. Refuses the dark developer site with a neon accent and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Sun-white ground #f7f6f2. Indigo #2b2f7f for all type, rules and primary controls. Madder #b23a65 and saffron #f2b31a only as silk and as project colour edges. Overlap chords #6a3d8f and #8a4a28 appear where bands cross. Jost light, wide-tracked capitals for display and labels. 1px indigo rules divide panels. Square controls with 2px radius. Dot-and-line rail menu.

STORY: The visitor sees who Favour is and that he builds web and mobile, sees real screens under the canopy, opens a project, then says hello or asks the AI.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark top left. Socials live in the rail, the footer and the contact page, so the silk keeps the top right clear. Dot-rail menu on the left edge. Three-line thin capital headline FAVOUR / ISRAEL / TAIWO on a white panel with a rounded lower right corner. Indigo, madder and saffron silk bands cross from the top left over a pale sky field on the right, drifting and mixing. Under them, a real web screen and phone screen. At right: one sentence and the buttons SEE MY WORK (filled indigo) and SAY HELLO (outlined). Lagos local time bottom left. A ruled row of featured project panels starts at the fold.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The silk canopy drifts on its own and leans toward the pointer; it moves as one cloth, and each crossing is painted in its exact chord colour (clipped overlap shapes, because multiply turns indigo over madder near black). Each project card carries one silk colour that sweeps across on hover.
