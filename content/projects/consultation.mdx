**AISHA Care Portal** is the professional side of the AISHA health platform. Verified doctors and healthcare partners use it to run remote consultations with patients across Africa, over secure video and real-time messaging.

## My Role

I built the frontend: the public landing page, the separate doctor and partner sign-in flows, and the portal screens professionals use once they are inside.

## Built Around How Doctors Work

One portal holds consultations, credential verification and the day-to-day tooling a remote practice needs. Doctors and healthcare partners get their own entry points, so each audience lands on the flow meant for them.

## Verified and Encrypted

Every professional is credential-checked before going live, and patient records stay encrypted end to end.

## Installable

The portal can be installed to a device for quick, app-like access.

## Key Features

- **Video Consultations**: secure doctor-to-patient video calls
- **Real-Time Messaging**: chat alongside or between consultations
- **Doctor Onboarding**: a "Get started as a doctor" sign-up path with credential verification
- **Partner Access**: a separate login and flow for healthcare partners
- **Installable Web App**: an add-to-device prompt for app-like access
